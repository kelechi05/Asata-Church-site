Our Story

Built By Faith, Kept By Grace

Our History

St. Bartholomew's Anglican Church, Enugu has grown as a worshipping family shaped by prayer, fellowship, service, and the steady proclamation of the Gospel. From its early gatherings to its present life as a parish community, the church has remained committed to raising disciples who love Christ and serve their neighbours with humility.

A Parish With A Living Witness

Across generations, members have gathered around Word and Sacrament, built ministries for children, youth, women, men, and families, and carried the light of Christ into the community. The story of the church is not only held in dates and buildings, but in lives changed, homes strengthened, and faith handed from one generation to the next.
