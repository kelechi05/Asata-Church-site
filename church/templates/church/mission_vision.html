Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Called To Worship, Sent To Serve

St. Bartholomew's Anglican Church exists to glorify God, build faithful disciples of Jesus Christ, and serve our community through worship, teaching, fellowship, evangelism, and compassionate care.

Mission

Making Christ Known

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers in the Anglican faith, and raise a Christ-centered community that lives by Scripture, prayer, love, and practical service.

Vision

A Faithful And Growing Church Family

To be a spiritually vibrant parish where people encounter God, grow in holiness, discover their gifts, and become witnesses of Christ's love in Enugu and beyond.

How We Live It Out

Worship

We gather around Word, Sacrament, prayer, praise, and reverence for God.

Discipleship

We help believers grow in Scripture, spiritual discipline, and Christlike character.

Fellowship

We build a caring parish family where every generation can belong and serve.

Evangelism

We share the Gospel with courage, humility, and practical love for our neighbours.

Our Continuing Commitment

We seek to remain Bible-based, Spirit-led, united, disciplined, and active in service. By God's grace, our mission and vision continue to shape every ministry, gathering, and act of care.
